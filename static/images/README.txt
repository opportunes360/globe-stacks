Globe Stacks photos (optional)

The home page shows photos if these two files exist in this folder:

  hero-1.jpg   the big banner at the top of the home page (wide photo, people look best on the right side)
  hero-2.jpg   the wide band halfway down the page ("Your story deserves a funded chapter")

If a file is missing, the site shows a blue design instead. Nothing breaks.

How to add a photo:
1. Download a free photo from unsplash.com or pexels.com (search: "african students", "graduation africa", "student laptop library").
   Choose one that is landscape (wider than tall) and has space for text. Avoid photos with logos or brand names.
2. Shrink it: open squoosh.app, drop the photo in, set width to 1600 pixels, format JPEG, quality about 70. Aim for under 300 KB.
3. Rename it exactly hero-1.jpg (or hero-2.jpg).
4. On GitHub open static/images, press Add file, then Upload files, drop the photo, Commit.

Credit the photographer if you like (not required by Unsplash or Pexels, but kind).

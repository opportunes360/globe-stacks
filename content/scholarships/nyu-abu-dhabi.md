## What it is

NYU Abu Dhabi (NYUAD) is a university in Abu Dhabi, in the United Arab Emirates. It does not run a separate scholarship competition for foreign students. Instead, it gives **need-based financial aid** to admitted undergraduates. You apply for admission first, and you ask for aid at the same time by filing a financial form called the **CSS Profile**.

The official page says NYUAD accepts applications for financial support from all students, regardless of citizenship. All grants for students who are not Emirati citizens are based on financial need.

Because this is aid and not a fixed scholarship, we have marked it **Partly funded**. The official pages say award amounts vary with each student's need. They do not promise that every admitted student gets full funding. Some students may receive a lot of support and others less, so plan for both.

## Is Tanzania included?

Yes. Aid is open to students of any citizenship, and there is no country list. There is no Tanzanian ministry or embassy step. You apply directly, online.

Two things we could **not confirm** on the official pages we read:

- Whether NYUAD admits students "need-blind" (without looking at your finances). Some other websites say it does, but the official pages we read do not say so. Do not rely on that claim.
- Whether aid is guaranteed to cover 100% of your need. The official pages say amounts vary.

If these matter to you, email the aid office: nyuad.financial.support@nyu.edu

## What it covers

The estimated cost of attendance for 2026-27 is **USD 90,434 a year**. The official page splits it like this:

- Tuition: USD 68,574
- Student health insurance: USD 2,416
- Books and supplies: USD 1,730
- Food: USD 5,800
- Housing: USD 6,000
- Personal costs: USD 2,364
- Travel: USD 3,550

Your grants are taken off the charges on your university bill each semester. The award letter may also include **personal support** and **travel support**. If your award includes travel support, the university books your flight, so do not book it yourself. If it does not include travel support, you pay for your own flight to Abu Dhabi.

Costs can change every year, and the health insurance fee can change in July. Students who get only part of their costs covered must pay the 5% VAT on food and health insurance themselves.

**Outside scholarships:** if you win another scholarship, you must tell the aid office. Together, your NYUAD aid and outside awards cannot be more than the cost of attendance, and NYUAD takes outside money into account when it sets your award.

## When to apply

| Round | Application | Financial aid form (CSS Profile) | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Early Decision I | 1 November | 10 November | 15 December |
| Early Decision II | 1 January | 10 January | 15 February |
| Regular Decision | 5 January | 1 February | 1 April |

All deadlines are 11:59 p.m. US Eastern time. The official pages do not print the year, so check the year on the official page. **As of 18 November 2026, Early Decision I has already passed.**

Early Decision is **binding**: if NYUAD admits you, you must go there. It is not easier to get in this way. Regular Decision is not binding. Early Decision applicants who are refused cannot apply again in the same round. Think carefully before choosing it, especially if you need to compare aid offers from other universities.

The CSS Profile must reach NYU **before** the admission decision dates. It is how NYUAD decides your aid, so a late form can mean no aid.

## How to apply

1. Choose your round (Early Decision II or Regular Decision are the ones still ahead as of 18 November 2026).
2. Apply for admission on the Common Application: <https://apply.commonapp.org/Login>. You must send:
   - the Common Application
   - your school records
   - one letter of recommendation
   - the STARS form (a self-reported record of your school results)
   - the NYU Abu Dhabi Applicant Profile
   - an English test result, if you need one (see below)
3. File the **CSS Profile** (school code **2785**) by the aid deadline: <https://student.collegeboard.org/css-financial-aid-profile>. The code must be on your form, or NYU will not see it.
4. If NYU's aid office asks for papers, upload them through the College Board's document service (IDOC). Send nothing unless they ask. Documents must be in English or come with an English translation, and money amounts must be converted into US dollars.
5. If you are admitted and you qualify for aid, your award letter comes with the admission decision, in the NYU student portal. Accepting admission also accepts the aid offered.

**CSS Profile fee:** USD 25 for the first university and USD 16 for each extra one. If the fee is a real hardship, you can ask for a free payment code. You can only ask after you have applied for admission and activated your NYU applicant portal. Codes are limited and go to the first people who ask.

**If your parents are separated, divorced or not married:** both parents must file their own CSS Profile. If you cannot get the other parent's information, NYU may accept a waiver form sent to ncpwaiver@nyu.edu by the same deadline, with proof. If you do not send either one, you can lose your chance of institutional aid for your whole time at NYUAD.

Keep a copy of everything you send. The aid office cannot give your CSS Profile back to you and cannot recover your password.

## Other rules that matter

- **Tests:** NYU is test-optional through the 2027-2028 application cycle. You do not have to send SAT or ACT scores.
- **English:** if English is not your first language, you generally need to send an English test result taken in the last two years. The exception is if all your classes for the three years before you apply were taught only in English. The official page says most students whose first language is not English should expect to send a test, even if they studied in English. Check the English test rules on the NYU page, because we did not find the score levels on the NYUAD pages we read.
- **Age:** you must be 17 by 1 August of the year you plan to enrol.
- **Maths:** many degrees at NYUAD need strong maths. The official page advises a solid base in calculus before you start.
- **Selection:** admission is highly selective. Some applicants are invited to a second step called Candidate Weekend. Candidate Weekend is by invitation only.
- **Renewal:** NYUAD does not look at your aid again each year. It usually renews at the same level as your first award, as long as you meet the university's renewal rules (satisfactory academic progress).

## Visa and living in the UAE

NYUAD arranges your entry permit for the UAE. You must have health insurance from the UAE to get the visa, and this is a required fee. Do not travel through the UAE before the start of the programme once the visa process has begun, because that could cancel your permit. Read our [student visa guide](/guides/student-visa-basics/) for the general steps.

## Prepare now

Aid for a university like this is competitive, so start early. Get your school results, certificates and ID documents ready. Work on your [personal statement](/guides/how-to-write-a-personal-statement/), and ask teachers for [recommendation letters](/guides/recommendation-letters-and-your-cv/) now. Your family will need to collect income and tax papers for the CSS Profile, and some papers may need translation into English.

Use only the official NYU Abu Dhabi website and the College Board for the CSS Profile. Never pay an agent to "guarantee" aid or admission. See our guide on [how to spot scholarship scams](/guides/how-scholarships-work-and-how-to-spot-scams/).

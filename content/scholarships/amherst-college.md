## What it is

Amherst College is a small liberal arts college in Massachusetts, USA. It does not run a separate scholarship competition. Instead, **every admitted student gets a need-based financial aid offer**, and Amherst pays most of it as a scholarship or grant that you never repay.

Three rules matter most for you:

- **Need-blind admission.** Amherst says it admits international students without looking at their level of financial need or whether they asked for aid.
- **Full need met.** Amherst says it meets 100% of calculated financial need for every admitted international student who applies for aid.
- **No loans in the first offer.** Amherst says its initial aid offers include no student loans.

Amherst is very selective. In one year its own aid FAQ reported 5,723 international applications and 272 admitted (the page does not give the year). This can be a real chance for a strong student, but it is not a sure award. Apply to other universities as well, such as the ones in our [scholarship list](/scholarships/).

## Is Tanzania included?

Yes, in the sense that Amherst accepts applicants from every country. Amherst says that, whatever your citizenship or location, you follow the same application process as any other student. The pages we read give no list of countries and no quota by country.

Amherst says its admission committee knows education systems around the world and looks at your grades, test scores if you send them, exam results or predictions, essays, recommendations, independent work and activities outside class. Amherst's pages do not say how Tanzanian national exams (Form Four and Form Six) are treated. **This is not confirmed.** Ask the admission office before you plan: admission@amherst.edu

## What it covers

Amherst says the amount depends on your family's financial situation, so **the aid may cover everything or only part of the cost**. Read this as "fully funded for families who need it", not as a promise of a free place for every student.

What Amherst says on its own pages:

- All Amherst aid is based on need. Amherst gives no merit, talent or sports scholarships.
- Aid offers usually include scholarship or grant money and a work opportunity (a campus job). All remaining need is met with scholarship or grants.
- Initial offers include no student loans.
- You can receive aid for a total of eight semesters. You must apply for renewal each year. Amherst says you must continue to have financial need.
- Outside scholarships you win must be reported, because Amherst counts them as resources.

The pages we read do **not** say how much the campus work expectation is, or whether flights and visa costs are included in the offer. **Not confirmed.** Ask the Office of Financial Aid before you accept an offer.

Amherst's list price is high, so the aid decision matters. We could not read the 2026-27 cost figures on Amherst's own page, so we do not print them here. Look them up on Amherst's website.

## When to apply

As of **18 November 2026**, for study starting in Fall 2027:

- **Early Decision closed on 9 November 2026.** It is a binding round, so you cannot use it now.
- **Regular Decision: 5 January 2027.** Decisions come by late March.
- **Financial aid applications for Regular Decision: 15 January 2027.**
- Amherst says aid forms sent after the priority date are still considered without penalty. Even so, send them early so your aid decision arrives with your admission decision.

For most Tanzanian students reading this now, the date that matters is **5 January 2027**. That is about seven weeks away, so start today.

## How to apply

1. Choose **one** application: the Common Application, the Apply Coalition with Scoir, or the QuestBridge application. Add Amherst's **Writing Supplement**.
2. Make sure your **school documents** reach Amherst: your school report and transcript, recommendations, and a mid-year report once your first-term grades are ready.
3. **SAT or ACT scores are optional** at Amherst for first-year applicants, domestic and international. Confirm this on Amherst's testing page before you decide not to send scores.
4. If English is not your first language, send an **English test** (see below).
5. Apply for **financial aid** at the same time (see below).
6. Application fee: reported as 75 US dollars. We could not confirm this or the fee waiver rules on a page we read directly. Check the First-Year Applicants page on amherst.edu.
7. Amherst does not accept applications from people who already hold a bachelor's degree.

## English test

Amherst says the only extra requirement for international applicants is proof of English. You must send scores from **TOEFL iBT (or TOEFL Home Edition), IELTS Academic or the Duolingo English Test** if English is not your first language and you have **not** had your most recent two years of school taught in English.

- Amherst strongly recommends a minimum of **TOEFL iBT 100** (taken before 21 January 2026) or **5.5** on the new scale, **IELTS Academic 7.5** overall, or **Duolingo 130**.
- Amherst accepts self-reported scores and official score reports.
- TOEFL code for Amherst: 3003.
- Amherst says waivers are not given on any other basis. It also says it gives no provisional admission and no English classes.

Many Tanzanian secondary schools teach in English, so you may qualify for the waiver, but **whether your school counts is not confirmed**. Ask admission@amherst.edu before you pay for a test.

## The financial aid application

- Fill in the **CSS Profile** for 2027-28 on the College Board website. Amherst's CSS Profile code is **3003**. Report your family's finances **in your home currency** (Tanzanian shillings).
- International students do **not** fill in the FAFSA.
- If the CSS Profile fee is a serious hardship, ask to complete the **International Student Financial Aid Application (ISFAA)** by logging into your Amherst admission account. Amherst treats it as the same as the CSS Profile and says it does not change your eligibility.
- Amherst then sends email instructions for its **Financial Aid Portal**. Wait for that email before you upload your ISFAA or income documents.
- If your parents are separated or divorced, each parent fills in their own CSS Profile or ISFAA.
- Income documents that are not in English need an English translation. Older versions of Amherst's page asked for this. Follow the current instructions in the portal.

Questions about aid go to the Office of Financial Aid at Amherst. Find its current contact details on the aid pages of amherst.edu.

## Beware of scams

Amherst will never ask you to pay to be considered for a scholarship. The only fees are the normal application fee and the test and CSS Profile fees paid to the testing companies. Only use the web addresses listed here (amherst.edu, commonapp.org, collegeboard.org). Read [how scholarships work and how to spot scams](/guides/how-scholarships-work-and-how-to-spot-scams/).

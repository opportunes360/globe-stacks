## What it is

Stipendium Hungaricum is the Hungarian government's scholarship for students from partner countries. You choose study programmes at Hungarian universities and apply through one online system.

## What it covers

The official page for the 2026/27 round lists: tuition-free education, a monthly stipend, an accommodation contribution, medical insurance and a student ID.

## When to apply

The 2026/27 round closed on **15 January 2026**. The round before it opened on **14 November 2025**. If the same pattern holds, the next round may open in mid-November 2026, but **we have not confirmed this**. Check the official news page every few days.

## How to apply

1. When the new round is announced, download the **Call for Applications** (bachelor's, master's and non-degree programmes) and the **Application Guide**.
2. In the Call, check that your country is on the list of partner countries and read how applicants from your country must apply.
3. Apply in the official system: <https://apply.stipendiumhungaricum.hu/>
4. Choose your programmes carefully. Each university has its own entry requirements.

Questions can go to the programme's contact email: stipendiumhungaricum@tpf.hu

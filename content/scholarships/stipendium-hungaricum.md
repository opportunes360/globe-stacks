## What it is

Stipendium Hungaricum is the Hungarian government's scholarship for students from partner countries. You choose study programmes at Hungarian universities and apply through one online system. The programme runs through agreements between Hungary and each partner country's government.

## Is Tanzania included?

Yes. Tanzania is on the official list of sending partners. For Tanzania the sending partner is the **Ministry of Education, Science and Technology** (Dodoma). The official Tanzania entry lists these study areas for bachelor's degrees:

- Agricultural Science
- Computer Science
- Information Technology
- Engineering Science
- Medical and Health Science
- Natural Science

You can only choose programmes that your country's agreement with Hungary allows, so check the Tanzania entry before you plan: <https://stipendiumhungaricum.hu/partners/?partner=tanzania>. It also gives the Ministry's contact details. The study areas can change from year to year.

## What it covers

The official site says tuition fees are covered and accommodation costs are supported. Earlier rounds also listed a monthly stipend, an accommodation contribution and medical insurance. **Check the exact amounts in the new Call.** They are not confirmed for 2027/28.

## When to apply

The 2026/27 round closed on **15 January 2026**. As of **18 November 2026** we could not find the 2027/28 Call published. Hungarian sources say it was not out yet. A university page expected it to open around November 2026, but **this is not confirmed**, so do not rely on any date until the official Call appears.

Tanzania's page says the Ministry's deadline follows the main programme deadline. Ministries can set earlier internal dates, so contact the Ministry as soon as the Call is out.

## How to apply

1. Watch the official news page every few days: <https://stipendiumhungaricum.hu/news>
2. When the new Call appears, read it fully, including the Application Guide.
3. Apply in the official online system: <https://apply.stipendiumhungaricum.hu/>. The application is free.
4. Also follow your sending partner's instructions. Applications must also reach the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology, and each country can ask for different documents and dates.
5. Choose your programmes carefully. Each university has its own entry requirements.

**Important:** if your sending partner does not nominate you, your application cannot go further. Apply early and keep in touch with the Ministry.

Applications are based on **nationality**, so you must hold Tanzanian citizenship. There is also an age rule. In the 2026/27 round, applicants had to be 18 years old by 31 August 2026. Check the exact rule in the new Call.

Questions about the programme can go to: stipendiumhungaricum@tpf.hu

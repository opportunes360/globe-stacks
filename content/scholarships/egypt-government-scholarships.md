## What it is

Egypt offers scholarships to students from partner countries, including Tanzania. Two routes matter for Tanzanian students:

- **EGYAID**, from the Egyptian Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research. The Egyptian ministry's letter to the Tanzanian Embassy in Cairo says applications go through the "Study in Egypt" platform, for undergraduate and postgraduate study.
- **Al-Azhar Al-Sharif**, the scholarships of Al-Azhar University in Cairo.

The Tanzanian Ministry of Education, Science and Technology publishes the call for Tanzanians. Its own list of foreign scholarships shows how small these schemes are: **25 places** for the Ministry of Higher Education route (Bachelor's only) and **50 places** for Al-Azhar (Bachelor's, Master's and PhD). It labels both as **partial scholarships**. The list gives usual application windows of May to July for the first and March to August for the second, with admission in September. The windows can change each year.

## Is Tanzania included?

Yes. The Egyptian ministry sent its notice to the Tanzanian Embassy in Cairo, and the Tanzanian Ministry of Education has announced these scholarships for "suitable Tanzanian candidates" in the 2025/26 round. It posted a notice for 2026/27 on 25 September 2026. Contact for the embassy: cairo@nje.go.tz.

## What it covers

For 2025/26 the Tanzanian Ministry's notice listed:

- Tuition fees.
- A monthly stipend, plus a book and thesis printing allowance for Master's and PhD students.
- A return ticket after you complete your studies.
- Medical treatment at university and government hospitals.

The same notice says **the Tanzanian Government will not give any financial support**, such as loans or grants, to candidates who win. You must find the rest of the money yourself. Students pay for travel date changes or cancellations, and for the return fare if the scholarship ends early. We read this text on copies of the notice posted on other websites, because the Ministry's own page for it did not open. **Check the wording on the Ministry's page before you rely on it.**

Not confirmed: the stipend amount, and whether housing is included. Websites that call these awards "fully funded" disagree with the Ministry's own "partial" label, so go with the Ministry.

## Language

The 2025/26 notice says Al-Azhar studies on the grant are taught in **Arabic**. Students who choose English or French pay all the extra costs. If you do not read Arabic, plan for this before you apply.

## When to apply

As of **18 November 2026**, we cannot give you an open deadline.

- **2025/26:** the Egyptian platform closed on 15 September 2025, and a copy of the Tanzanian Ministry's notice gives 15 August 2025 for the Tanzanian route.
- **2026/27:** the Ministry of Education posted its notice on 25 September 2026. The page text did not load for us, so we could not confirm its deadline. Some websites say 15 August 2026, but we could not match that to an official page.
- **2027/28:** no call found yet.

Past rounds ran roughly from the middle of the year to August or September. Watch the Ministry's announcements page from about April 2027: <https://www.moe.go.tz/en>. Do not rely on a date from a social media post or a blog.

## How to apply

1. Read the current Ministry of Education notice in full when it appears.
2. Register and apply online on the **Study in Egypt** platform: <https://admission.study-in-egypt.gov.eg>. This is the only application route the notices name.
3. Prepare your documents early. For 2025/26 the Ministry asked for:
   - your Certificate of Secondary Education (CSEE) or equivalent,
   - your Advanced Certificate (ACSEE) or equivalent,
   - for Master's and PhD, your original degree certificates and transcripts,
   - your birth certificate.
4. Each of these documents must be **certified by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and East African Cooperation and approved by the Egyptian Embassy**, and carry the seals. This takes time, so start early.
5. Your name must be written the same way on every document.
6. Language of documents: copies of the 2025/26 notice disagree. One says all documents must be in Arabic. Another says Arabic, English or French, or a certified translation. Follow the Ministry's current notice.

**Not confirmed:** an age limit, a religion requirement (one blog says Al-Azhar candidates must be Muslim, but we found no official text), and how winners are chosen.

Questions about the Egyptian side go to the Tanzanian Embassy in Cairo: cairo@nje.go.tz, tel. +202 33 374 155.

## Beware of scams

The Egyptian platform and the Tanzanian Ministry do not ask you to pay anyone to be selected. Many websites copy these notices and add wrong dates, so check every notice on moe.go.tz or the embassy site.

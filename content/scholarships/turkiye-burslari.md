## What it is

Türkiye Scholarships is the Turkish government's scholarship for international students. It is run by the Presidency for Turks Abroad and Related Communities (YTB). You make **one online application** and you do not apply to universities yourself. If you are selected, the programme places you in a university and department in Türkiye.

It offers associate degrees (two years), bachelor's degrees, master's degrees and PhDs. This page focuses on the **bachelor's** scholarship, because Globe Stacks is for undergraduates.

## Is Tanzania included?

Yes. The official Criteria page says the scholarship is open to **citizens of all countries**, and the 2026 announcement says applications were open "for international students from all countries". Tanzania is not on a special list and you do not need a sponsor or a nomination from any office. You apply directly to YTB.

The only people who cannot apply are Turkish citizens (including people who lost Turkish citizenship) and people already studying at the same level in a Turkish university.

We did not find any official page with rules written only for Tanzania. If you are unsure how the rules apply to your Tanzanian certificates, ask YTB before you apply (see "Questions" below).

## Who can apply (bachelor's)

These are the official basic criteria:

- **Age:** under 21 years old. The official page does not state the exact birth-date cutoff, so confirm it when the round opens.
- **Academic achievement:** at least **70%**. For **Medicine, Dentistry and Pharmacy** the minimum is **90%**.
- **Graduation:** you must have graduated, or be graduating at the end of the current academic year (before August).
- **Citizenship:** not a Turkish citizen.

The official pages do not explain how a Tanzanian ACSEE (Form Six) result is turned into a percentage. Do not guess. Ask YTB if you are close to the limit.

## What it covers

The official Full-Time Scholarships page lists:

- university and department placement
- tuition fee
- accommodation
- health insurance
- a one-year Turkish language course
- a once-off flight ticket at the start of your studies and one when you graduate
- a **monthly stipend**: **6,500 TL for undergraduates** (9,500 TL for master's and 13,000 TL for PhD)

The stipend is in Turkish lira and the amount can change, so check the official page before you rely on it.

**Merit Scholarship:** a few students get a stipend that is **twice** the normal amount. For a bachelor's degree you need an average of at least 90% **and** at least one of these: a high SAT score (1350, or 1400 for engineering) or an IB score of 33 or more, together with TOEFL iBT 80 or IELTS 6.5; a place in the top 10 in your national exam; or a gold, silver or bronze medal in a recognised international science olympiad. Read the full table on the official page: <https://www.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr/fulltimeprograms>

## Language you will study in

Many programmes in Turkish universities are taught in **Turkish**. Some are taught in English or other languages, and you can see the language of each programme while you choose in the application system.

If you do not already have a **C1 Turkish certificate**, you must take the one-year Turkish language course and pass C1 by the end of that year. This is true **even if your degree is taught in English**. If you choose an English programme, you may also need to show an accepted English certificate, such as TOEFL, if the university asks for one.

## When to apply

Every year the general application period is **10 January to 20 February**. The 2026 round ran on those dates. As of **18 November 2026** the official news page shows no announcement for 2027. The 2026 announcement came out on 29 December 2025, so watch the site from late December. Do not rely on a date until YTB publishes it.

The official yearly calendar is:

- **10 January to 20 February:** applications
- **March to April:** evaluation
- **April to June:** interviews
- **Early August:** results
- **August:** initial procedures
- **September:** awardees travel to Türkiye

The application system is busy near the deadline, so do not wait for the last days.

Other programmes (the Success Scholarship, the Research Scholarship and others) have different periods and are announced separately. They are not the main bachelor's route.

## How to apply

1. Read the **Criteria & Scholarship Programs** page: <https://www.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr/scholarshipsprograms>
2. Search for programmes with the search tool on the official website, so you know which departments and languages exist before the round opens.
3. When the round opens, register on the official application system (TBBS): <https://tbbs.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr/>. The application is **online and free**. It can be done in eight languages, including English and French.
4. Fill in the form, choose your universities and departments, and upload your documents.
5. Submit before the deadline.

**Documents to upload:**

- a valid identity document or passport
- a photograph taken within the last year
- your national exam results, if any
- your diploma or a temporary graduation certificate
- your transcript
- international exam results (for example SAT) and language test results (for example TOEFL), only if your chosen university or programme requires them

If you hold international qualifications such as SAT or GCSE instead of national exam scores, the official page says you must show the minimum results needed to complete secondary school under those exams.

## How you are selected

1. **Preliminary check:** age, minimum academic achievement and documents.
2. **Expert evaluation:** a committee looks at your academic record, your interests and career goals, whether your choices make sense together, your letter of intent and your social activities.
3. **Test and interview (undergraduates):** a 30-question quantitative test of mathematics, geometry and logic, with 60 minutes to finish. The interview is on the same day. It usually lasts 15 to 30 minutes and covers why you applied, your academic knowledge and your career goals. Bring the original documents and certificates of your latest education. YTB publishes sample test questions with an answer key: <https://tbbsweb.azureedge.net/tbbsweb/Page/TurkiyeScholarhips/EvaluationAndProcess/Sample-Questions.pdf>
4. **Final decision:** a selection committee makes the list of awardees.

The test is written with the Turkish curriculum in mind, so practise the sample questions. Candidates for science, engineering and health programmes are expected to understand English questions.

## Keeping the scholarship

The scholarship has rules about academic success, language, leave of absence, changing department and study time, and money can be reduced or stopped if you break them. YTB publishes a Scholarship Procedures guide: <https://www.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr/scholarshipsprocess>

## Questions

YTB call centre: **0 850 455 0 982**. Email: **info@turkiyeburslari.gov.tr**. Both are listed on the official Contact page: <https://www.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr/about/contact>. If you call from Tanzania, check the country dialling code first.

> **Careful:** the application is free and made only online. Never pay anyone who promises to "guarantee" a place or offers to apply for you. Read [how to spot scams](/guides/how-scholarships-work-and-how-to-spot-scams/).

## Prepare now

Get your certificates and transcripts in order, make sure your [passport](/guides/getting-your-passport-in-tanzania/) is valid, start your [personal statement](/guides/how-to-write-a-personal-statement/), and think about the activities you can describe for the social activities part. Practise the sample test questions. Explore the programme search so you know which departments you want and what language they use.

## Our honest note

This is a very competitive programme. Students from all over the world apply for a limited number of places. Apply with a strong profile, and apply to other scholarships too.

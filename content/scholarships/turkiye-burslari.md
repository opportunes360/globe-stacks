## What it is

Türkiye Burslari is run by the Turkish government. It lets you apply once, online, and the programme places you at a university in Türkiye if you win.

## What it covers

According to the official announcement, the scholarship includes financial support, tuition fees, accommodation and health insurance, plus university placement.

## When to apply

Applications are received in **one period each year**. In 2026 it ran from 10 January to 20 February. We have not seen the 2027 dates yet, so check the official site often from December. Last time it opened in January.

## How to apply

1. Read the **Criteria and Scholarship Programs** page on the official site. It lists the age limit, grade requirements and how you are scored.
2. Create an account on the official application system: <https://tbbs.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr/>
3. Prepare your documents *before* the round opens: passport, certificates, transcripts, a clear photo and a personal statement. See our guides on the [personal statement](/guides/how-to-write-a-personal-statement/) and the [passport](/guides/getting-your-passport-in-tanzania/).

> **Careful:** the application is free. Never pay anyone who promises to "guarantee" a place. Read [how to spot scams](/guides/how-scholarships-work-and-how-to-spot-scams/).

## Our honest note

This is a very competitive programme with applicants from all over the world. Apply with a strong profile, and apply to other scholarships too.

## What it is

The MEXT scholarship is paid for by Japan's Ministry of Education. The undergraduate track covers a bachelor's degree in natural science or social science at a Japanese university.

## The last round in Tanzania

The Embassy of Japan in Tanzania ran the 2027 round with these dates:

- Applications: 21 April to 21 May 2026.
- Written exam: 3 June 2026. Interview: 23 June 2026 (both "subject to change").
- The embassy only does the first screening. The final decision is made in Japan, and a recommendation does not guarantee selection.

The next round is not announced. If it follows the same pattern, it would open in about April 2027, but **please confirm on the embassy page**.

## Who could apply last time

- Tanzanian nationals.
- Under 25 (born on or after 2 April 2002).
- Completed 12 years of school education or an equivalent qualification. Students who are about to graduate were included.

## How to apply (last round's process)

1. Read the undergraduate guidelines on the embassy page.
2. Download and complete the application documents.
3. Put all documents and the checklist in one envelope, with the coversheet on top and **no staples**.
4. Post or hand-deliver it to the Embassy of Japan in Dar es Salaam. **Email is not accepted.**

## Use the waiting time well

Application packs are long and strict. Start reading last year's guidelines now, practise for the written exam, and prepare your [personal statement](/guides/how-to-write-a-personal-statement/) and [recommendation letters](/guides/recommendation-letters-and-your-cv/).

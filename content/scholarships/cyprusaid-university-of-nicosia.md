## What it is

The University of Nicosia (UNIC) runs this scholarship scheme together with **CyprusAid** and the **Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Cyprus**. It is for students from selected countries who want to study at UNIC in Cyprus. The announcement on UNIC's website is dated 12 June 2026.

The scheme also has a few postgraduate places (selected Master's programmes and one PhD). Globe Stacks covers undergraduate study only, so this entry is about the bachelor's scholarships.

## Is Tanzania included?

Yes. The official UNIC announcement lists ten eligible countries: Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda, Zambia, Botswana, Mauritius, Ghana, Senegal, **Tanzania** and Madagascar.

To show you are eligible, you must hold a valid passport or official identification document that confirms your nationality from one of these countries.

## Which programmes?

The bachelor's scholarship applies to **English-taught, on-campus Bachelor's programmes** at the University of Nicosia. **Programmes offered by the Medical School are not included.**

The announcement does not list the individual programmes. Check what UNIC offers here: <https://www.unic.ac.cy/bachelor-degrees/>. Then ask the International Admissions Office to confirm that your chosen programme is covered before you apply.

## What it covers

The official page says the scholarship covers:

- 100% of the tuition fees for the approved programme of study
- Accommodation
- A monthly sponsorship allowance
- Medical examinations
- Visa or residence permit fees
- Insurance
- Other personal or administrative expenses

**Not confirmed:** the amount of the monthly allowance, and the exact limits of "other personal or administrative expenses". The page does not say whether flights to Cyprus are included, so do not assume they are. Ask the International Admissions Office for these details in writing.

## Who can apply

You must:

- Hold nationality from one of the eligible countries (Tanzania is one)
- Meet the University of Nicosia's admission requirements for your programme
- Meet the English language requirements of your programme
- Submit all the documents needed for admission and for the scholarship evaluation

The page does not set one English test score for everyone, and it does not state an age limit or a minimum grade. Those are **not confirmed**. Check the entry requirements on your programme's page.

## Documents

Undergraduate applicants will be asked for documents such as:

- A valid passport
- Your secondary school leaving certificate
- Academic transcripts, where applicable
- Proof of English language proficiency
- Any other documents needed for admission and for your visa

UNIC decides exactly what it needs, and the page does not say which Tanzanian certificates it accepts, so confirm this with the Admissions Office. If your passport is not ready, start now: [getting your passport in Tanzania](/guides/getting-your-passport-in-tanzania/).

## When to apply

The **Spring 2027 intake** deadline on the official page was **6 November 2026**. That date has passed.

Some third-party scholarship websites show a later date (4 December 2026) for Spring 2027. **The University of Nicosia's own page does not say this**, so do not rely on it. If you missed 6 November and still want Spring 2027, email the International Admissions Office and ask whether late applications are accepted. Do not assume they are.

As of **18 November 2026** the official page does not announce a later intake (for example Fall 2027). Watch the UNIC news page: <https://www.unic.ac.cy/news/>

## How to apply

The official page gives one route: **email**.

1. Choose an eligible English-taught bachelor's programme (not Medical School).
2. Prepare your documents (see above).
3. Email **int.admissions@unic.ac.cy** with the subject **'CyprusAid' scholarship scheme**.
4. UNIC assesses your application against its admission requirements and the scholarship terms. You are told the result **electronically**.

Contact details from the official page:

- Email: int.admissions@unic.ac.cy
- Telephone: +357 22 841528
- Website: <https://www.unic.ac.cy/>

The official page does not mention any application fee. Only use the official email address above, never pay an agent to "secure" a place, and read [how to spot scholarship scams](/guides/how-scholarships-work-and-how-to-spot-scams/).

## After you are accepted

You will need a visa or residence permit for Cyprus. The scholarship covers visa or residence permit fees, but you still need to prepare the paperwork. Read [student visa basics](/guides/student-visa-basics/) to know what to expect.

## Prepare now

Even though the Spring 2027 date has passed, a next intake may come. Get your certificates and transcripts scanned and ready, check your English level, start your [personal statement](/guides/how-to-write-a-personal-statement/), and ask your teachers for [recommendation letters](/guides/recommendation-letters-and-your-cv/) early.

## What it is

The Kingdom of Morocco offers scholarships to students from partner countries through the **Moroccan Agency for International Cooperation (AMCI)**. Each country gets a quota. For Tanzania the quota in the call we read was **30 scholarships** to study at Moroccan public universities, technical institutes and vocational centres.

Tanzania's Ministry of Education, Science and Technology also lists this scholarship (Morocco Government Scholarship, quota 30, bachelor's level, **partial** scholarship, application May to July, admission in September).

This is **not** a full scholarship for Tanzanians. Read "What it covers" before you plan.

## Is Tanzania included?

Yes. Morocco gave Tanzania a quota of 30 places, and Tanzanian ministries publish a call each year. The most recent Tanzanian call we could read was published by the **Ministry of Education and Vocational Training, Zanzibar**, for the **2024/25** academic year. It says the scholarship is open to "eligible Tanzanian nationals". We did not find a Tanzanian call for 2025/26, 2026/27 or 2027/28 on the official sites we checked. **We do not know whether the 2027/28 call will follow the same rules.**

## What it covers

From the 2024/25 Tanzanian call:

- **Full tuition fees**
- **A monthly allowance of 750 dirhams** (the notice says about 75 US dollars)

You pay for:

- Return air travel
- Registration fees
- Accommodation
- Transport inside Morocco

You must also take out **health insurance** for your stay. The call says the policy should include medical care, reimbursement of medical costs, evacuation to Tanzania in case of serious illness, and repatriation in case of death.

The call also says there is **no loan or grant from the Government of Tanzania** for candidates who win this scholarship. Plan how your family will pay for flights, housing and daily costs before you apply.

Other countries' 2026/27 calls list different terms. For example, Saint Lucia's government published a call with a monthly stipend of 1,750 dirhams, flights and insurance. **Do not assume these terms apply to Tanzania.** Tanzania's terms are the ones in the Tanzanian call.

## Who can apply

From the 2024/25 Tanzanian call, for bachelor's study:

- Completed the **Advanced Certificate of Secondary Education Examination (ACSEE)** in 2023 or 2024 and passed with **Division I or II** (or a diploma with at least Upper Second Class)
- **Not older than 23** by August of the year of entry
- **Not already admitted** to any higher education institution in Morocco
- A **medical certificate** from a public hospital saying you have no contagious illness

For Master's and PhD, the call asks for a first degree with at least Upper Second Class (Master's) or a recognised Master's (PhD). Globe Stacks lists undergraduate scholarships only, so this page focuses on the bachelor's route.

For the new call, check the ACSEE years and age limit. They change each year.

**Language:** the call says the language of instruction is **French**, except Arabic Language and Literature and Islamic Studies, which are taught in Arabic. A certificate of French training is an advantage. If you do not speak French, start learning now.

## When to apply

We found **no open call for Tanzanians** as of **18 November 2026**. Other countries' calls for 2026/27 closed between May and July 2026, and Tanzania's own list gives May to July as the usual application window, so the next Tanzanian call is likely to come in the first half of 2027. **This is not confirmed.** Do not rely on any date until a call is published.

The 2024/25 call allowed only a few weeks between publication and the deadline (12 May 2024). Have your documents ready in advance.

## How to apply

1. Watch the websites of the **Ministry of Education, Science and Technology** (moe.go.tz) and the **Ministry of Education and Vocational Training, Zanzibar** (moez.go.tz) for the new call. The Zanzibar notice is here: <https://moez.go.tz/scholarships/154-partial-scholarships-tenable-in-morocco-for-the-academic-year-2024-2025>
2. Read the list of Moroccan institutions and what they teach. The call points to a catalogue (in French) and to these Moroccan sites: amci.ma, enssup.gov.ma and men.gov.ma.
3. Download the AMCI application form. The 2024/25 call gave this link: <http://www.amci.ma/fcf.pdf>
4. Prepare **two copies** of each document:
   - Completed application form
   - Certified copies of academic certificates, transcripts and birth certificate
   - Certified copy of the passport data page
   - Certified copy of your national ID (NIDA)
   - Recent police clearance certificate
   - Medical certificate from a public hospital
   - Two recent colour passport photos, with your name, surname and nationality written on the back
5. Send the complete file to the ministry named in the call. In 2024/25 this was the Permanent Secretary, Ministry of Education and Vocational Training, P.O. Box 394, Zanzibar. Check the new call for the address. It may differ.

Morocco's own system says foreign students apply through their home country's government. AMCI only considers files that arrive through that route, so you cannot apply straight to AMCI.

**If you win:** the winners get a letter through official channels with details of the programme. The place is fixed, and Moroccan calls say requests to change school or direction are not accepted. Bring the letter to AMCI in Rabat to finish registration. Other countries' calls describe this step. We have not confirmed it for Tanzania.

## Beware of scams

The official call we read does not mention any fee to apply. Be careful of any agent, WhatsApp group or website that asks for money to "secure" a Moroccan scholarship. Use only the ministry sites named above. Read [how scholarships work and how to spot scams](/guides/how-scholarships-work-and-how-to-spot-scams/).

## Prepare now

Get your certified copies, police clearance and medical certificate ready, and apply for your [passport](/guides/getting-your-passport-in-tanzania/) now if you do not have one. Start learning French. Think about a [student visa](/guides/student-visa-basics/) later, once you have a place. Keep your ACSEE results safe.

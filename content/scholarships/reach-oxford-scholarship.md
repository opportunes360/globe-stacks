## What it is

The Reach Oxford Scholarship (formerly the Oxford Student Scholarship) is paid by the University of Oxford in the UK. It is for students from low-income countries who, for political or financial reasons, or because suitable education is not available, cannot study for a degree in their own countries.

Two things matter most before you read on:

- **You cannot apply for this scholarship by itself.** You must first apply to Oxford and receive an offer of a place. Oxford says it cannot consider scholarship applications from students who have no offer.
- **It is very small and very competitive.** Oxford gives around 2 to 3 awards a year. The University says it has no other funding for candidates who are not chosen.

## Is Tanzania included?

Yes. Tanzania is on the official list. Eligibility is based on **nationality**: you must be a citizen of a country that receives official development assistance from the OECD's Development Assistance Committee. Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda, Burundi, Ethiopia, Mozambique, Zambia, Malawi, Zimbabwe and many other African countries are also on the list.

Other rules from the official page:

- Undergraduate level only, for new students.
- All courses in Oxford's course list **except medicine**.
- Oxford looks for candidates of "the highest academic ability". Financial need, social commitment and not being able to study the degree in your own country are also major criteria.
- You should intend to return to your country after your studies.
- Priority goes to students who have not studied at undergraduate level before.

Not every Oxford college offers Reach Oxford Scholarships each year. If your college does not take part, you can still apply and win. If you succeed, Oxford will arrange for you to move to a college that does.

## What it covers

The official page says the scholarship gives:

- **Course fees**
- **A grant for living costs**
- **One return air fare per year**

It lasts for 3 or 4 years, depending on how long your course is. The page does not say how much the living grant is. **Check the amount when Oxford publishes the 2027 details.**

After you win, you must write an annual report on your studies and your social activities and achievements at Oxford. The award continues each year only if your academic progress is satisfactory.

## When to apply

This is where many Tanzanian students get caught, so read it carefully.

- **Oxford's own application deadline for 2027 entry has already passed.** It closed at 6 p.m. UK time on **15 October 2026**. Oxford does not take late applications. Offers for 2027 entry are sent on **12 January 2027**.
- **The scholarship deadline for 2027 entry is 26 January 2027.** Only students who received an offer can apply. Oxford says the selection takes place in April 2027, and applicants will hear by the end of May 2027.
- Oxford will publish the 2027 scholarship instructions on the official page **in January 2027**. The exact closing time on 26 January and the documents needed are **not confirmed** yet.

**If you did not apply to Oxford by 15 October 2026:** you cannot use the 2027 round. Your next chance is **2028 entry**. Oxford has used a mid-October deadline for years (15 October for 2027 entry), but the date for 2028 entry is **not confirmed**. Check the official admissions page from mid-2027: <https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/undergraduate/applying-to-oxford/admissions-timeline>

**If you have a deferred offer:** apply for the scholarship in January of the year you will start. For example, apply in January 2027 if you start in October 2027. Oxford can consider an undergraduate scholarship application **only once**.

**If you are applying for Oxford's Astrophoria Foundation Year:** apply for the scholarship in January of the year you will start your degree course, after the Foundation Year. Oxford will contact you during the Foundation Year to confirm which scholarships you can apply for. Check the official page for the full rule.

## How to apply

1. Choose your Oxford course. Medicine is not eligible for this scholarship.
2. Apply to Oxford through UCAS by Oxford's deadline. Check whether your course needs an admissions test or written work. Oxford interviews are online for 2027 entry. Read the Guide for applicants first: <https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/undergraduate/applying-to-oxford/guide>
3. Make sure you meet the English language rules (see below) and the entry requirements for your course.
4. If Oxford offers you a place, apply for the Reach Oxford Scholarship in January. Follow the instructions on the official scholarship page and do not rely on other websites for the form or dates.
5. Wait for the result. Oxford says it will tell applicants by the end of May 2027 (for 2027 entry).

Oxford warns that it is not responsible for outside websites that advertise this scheme. Use only ox.ac.uk, and read [how scholarships work and how to spot scams](/guides/how-scholarships-work-and-how-to-spot-scams/).

## Entry requirements to check

- **Academic requirements:** each course page lists the grades and subjects Oxford expects. Oxford accepts a wide range of international qualifications, but we could not confirm on Oxford's own pages how it treats the Tanzanian ACSEE. This is **not confirmed**. Check Oxford's international qualifications guidance (ox.ac.uk/intquals) and use the contact details on Oxford's undergraduate admissions pages if you are unsure.
- **English language:** Oxford says its undergraduate courses are demanding and you must show a high level of English unless an exemption applies. Oxford recommends the IELTS Academic test. It says IELTS General, IELTS Life Skills and IELTS Online are not accepted. Check the required scores and the exemption rules on the official page: <https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/undergraduate/applying-to-oxford/for-international-students/english-language-requirements>
- **Student visa:** if you are offered a place, you will need a UK student visa. See our [student visa basics](/guides/student-visa-basics/) guide and Oxford's student immigration pages.

## Prepare now

The gap between Oxford's decision day (12 January) and the scholarship deadline (26 January) is only two weeks, so get ready early. Collect your certificates, results and ID, keep your [passport](/guides/getting-your-passport-in-tanzania/) valid, and ask your teachers for [recommendation letters](/guides/recommendation-letters-and-your-cv/) in good time. Start working on your [personal statement](/guides/how-to-write-a-personal-statement/) well before Oxford's mid-October deadline. Book an English test early, because places can fill up.

Questions about the scheme should go to Oxford's undergraduate admissions office. Use the contact details on the official page, not those on other websites.

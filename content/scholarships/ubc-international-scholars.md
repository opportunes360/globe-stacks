## What it is

The International Scholars Program is the University of British Columbia's (UBC) main financial-aid programme for international undergraduates. It is for students with outstanding grades and leadership who also **need meaningful financial help** to attend. UBC says it has supported more than 680 Scholars from more than 111 countries since 2001, and its page features a Scholar from Tanzania.

Scholars also join a community with workshops, retreats, adviser support and the option to take part in exchange and co-op programmes.

## Is Tanzania included?

The programme is open to international students from any country, and UBC does not publish a list of eligible countries. Tanzania is on UBC's country list for admission requirements, which means UBC has published requirements for Tanzanian school results. **We could not read UBC's Tanzania-specific grade requirements**, because they load only after you pick your country on this page: <https://you.ubc.ca/applying-ubc/requirements/international-high-schools/>. Open it, choose Tanzania and read the requirements before you start.

Two rules matter a lot for Tanzanian applicants:

- **The graduation date.** For the 2027 Winter Session you must have graduated from high school **no earlier than June 2026**. The page does not say how this applies to Form Six and the ACSEE calendar. If you finished Form Six in 2026, ask UBC to confirm that you qualify before you spend time on the application. If you finished in 2025 or earlier, you are not eligible.
- **The study permit.** The page says you must be an international student "studying in Canada on a Canadian study permit." UBC's own site puts the study permit step under "After you've been admitted," so you do not need the permit before you apply. See our [student visa guide](/guides/student-visa-basics/) for the basics.

## Who can apply

You must meet **all** of these. UBC says there are no exceptions.

- You are an international student entering UBC directly from an accredited secondary school.
- You are applying for your **first undergraduate degree**.
- You can prove **significant financial need**. UBC reviews official documents on your family's gross income, expenses and assets. Your family must not have access to other funding that covers a significant part of your costs.
- You meet UBC's general and programme-specific admission requirements, and you have superior grades (all As or the equivalent).
- You meet UBC's English Language Admission Standard. If you do not, you may be able to join Vantage College and be considered for the Vantage One Excellence Award.

**These degrees are not eligible:** Bachelor of Nursing (Okanagan and Vancouver), Bachelor of Dental Science in Dental Hygiene, Bachelor of Medical Laboratory Science, Bachelor + Master of Management, dual degrees, joint degrees between UBC Okanagan and UBC Vancouver, and joint degrees with other institutions.

**You cannot apply for both aid routes.** If you apply to this programme, you will not be considered for UBC's merit-based entrance awards. Read the merit awards first (<https://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/scholarships-awards-international-students/international-entrance-scholarships/>) and decide which route fits you.

## What it covers

UBC says the awards **range in value up to the full cost of your programme and living expenses**, and that the value of each award depends on your financial circumstances. The official pages do not give a fixed amount, so we cannot tell you what a Tanzanian student will receive. That is why this entry says "Partly funded": a full award is possible, but it is not promised. You do not choose an award. UBC considers you automatically for the ones you qualify for:

- **Karen McKellin International Leader of Tomorrow Award:** for superior grades and strong leadership that supports others, with school and community service.
- **Donald A. Wehrung International Student Award:** for students from impoverished or war-torn areas who did well in hard conditions and could not attend university without major help.
- **International Impact Award:** for students who lead on sustainability and climate, natural resources, social justice, equity and inclusion, or health and wellness. First-generation learners and students from diverse backgrounds get priority.
- **Vantage One Excellence Award:** for students who do not yet meet the English standard for direct entry. It ranges up to the full cost of the programme and living expenses.

## When to apply

- **2 December 2026, 12:00 p.m. Pacific Time:** deadline to submit your International Scholars application. In Tanzania this is **11:00 p.m. on 2 December** (our conversion: Vancouver is on standard time in December). UBC advises submitting well before the deadline in case of technical problems.
- **31 January 2027:** your admission documents must reach UBC, and you must meet the English standard by this date. UBC cannot proceed if documents are missing.
- **January to March 2027:** some applicants are invited to a video interview and get one week to record answers.
- **February to April 2027:** results come out on a rolling basis. UBC says you will know by early to mid April.
- **1 May 2027:** housing application deadline. UBC asks you to wait for the award decision before applying for residence.

The deadline was 15 November until UBC extended it. Some websites still show the old date, so trust UBC's page.

**If the deadline (2 December 2026) is close, be realistic.** The [deadlines page](/deadlines/) shows the days left. You need a school nominator and a teacher referee to finish their forms a few days before the deadline, plus four essays and detailed family financial documents. If you cannot finish in time, check UBC's page for the next round. As of 18 November 2026 UBC has not announced dates for the September 2028 intake, so do not rely on any date until it appears.

## How to apply

1. Choose your programme and campus (Vancouver or Okanagan). UBC considers you for your first-choice programme, and you should still list a second choice.
2. **Ask your school to nominate you now.** You need one nominator (a guidance counsellor, principal or administrator) and one teacher referee, and they must be two different people. A school or registered non-profit can nominate up to **two students for UBC Vancouver** (including Vantage College) and **one for UBC Okanagan**, so ask your school whether it has already used its places. A non-profit can nominate a student only if it acts as a school in place of one, or runs an education and mentoring programme for high-school graduates from less fortunate backgrounds.
3. Create your account on the award application page: <https://undergraduatescholarships.ubc.ca/prog/2027_international_scholars_program_application/>. Invite your nominator and referee. You cannot finish your application until both have submitted their forms, so follow up with them.
4. Gather your family's income, expenses, assets, dependants, school fees and other sources of support. Fill in your activities and awards, and write **four essays** of about 150 to 250 words each.
5. Apply for admission to UBC. UBC says the admissions application on the EducationPlannerBC website was having technical problems, so you can start the scholarship application first and finish the admissions application when it becomes available. The award application needs your UBC student number.
6. Submit your International Scholars application by the deadline, then send your documents by 31 January 2027. UBC will email you ("Your UBC Application – Next Steps") with what to send. Check your spam folder and mark UBC emails as safe.

Questions go to UBC through this form: <https://account.you.ubc.ca/s/ask-ubc>. Nobody can sell you a place in this programme. Read our guide on [how to spot scholarship scams](/guides/how-scholarships-work-and-how-to-spot-scams/).

## Prepare now

Start your [personal statement](/guides/how-to-write-a-personal-statement/) work early, because the essays ask about your leadership and community involvement. Ask your teacher referee and nominator for [recommendation letters](/guides/recommendation-letters-and-your-cv/) today. Collect your Form Four and Form Six certificates and results, and ask your family for the financial papers UBC will want. Check the English language rules on UBC's page, because you may need a test result in time for 31 January.

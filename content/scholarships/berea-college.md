## What it is

Berea College is a small liberal arts college in Berea, Kentucky, USA. It only offers bachelor's degrees. For international students, Berea's official page says that all admitted students receive financial aid and scholarships that cover **100% of tuition, housing, food and fees**. You apply once, and the same application is used for admission and for aid.

This is a **very competitive** route. Berea says it receives hundreds of applications from well-qualified students around the world and can enrol fewer than 40 new international students each year.

## Is Tanzania included?

Berea does not publish a list of eligible countries, and its page does not mention Tanzania by name. International applicants from any country can apply. Berea wants students from all parts of the world, and says it is rare for more than one student from any country to be offered admission in a year. That means you compete with other Tanzanians for one place at most, so make your application as strong as you can.

## What it covers

- Tuition, housing, food and fees: **100% covered** for all admitted international students.
- **Work Program:** every student works at least 10 hours a week on campus. Most international students work 15 to 20 hours a week and earn over $2,000 during the school year. International students can take part without a work permit. Summer jobs are available, and you can stay on campus in holidays and summers. Berea encourages you to save from this money for your personal expenses.
- **Deposit:** admitted students must pay **$2,200** to confirm enrolment. It can be used for certain expenses during your four years. If you cannot pay all or part of it, Berea says extra aid is available and your situation is considered.

**Not confirmed:** whether flights and the US visa fee are covered. The official page does not say. Ask Berea before you plan your budget.

## Who can apply

According to Berea's page:

- You must meet the university entrance requirements in your own country and have **outstanding secondary or leaving exam results**. The page does not name Tanzanian exams. For most Tanzanian applicants this means Form Six (ACSEE) results, but ask Berea to confirm what they expect from a Tanzanian school record.
- You must send an approved English test score (see below). There are no exceptions, even if you already speak English.
- Admission looks at academic results, **financial need**, ambition and sense of purpose, and recommendations.
- You **cannot** apply if you already have a bachelor's degree.
- New international students can only start in the **Fall Term**. There is no spring start.

## English test scores

All applicants must send a score from one of these tests. Minimum scores:

| Test | Minimum score |
| --- | --- |
| IELTS | 6 overall, 5 in each area |
| TOEFL | 68 (internet-based), 190 (computer-based) or 520 (paper-based) |
| Duolingo English Test | 95 |
| SAT | 980 combined |
| ACT | 19 composite |

Berea will almost never accept a score from a test taken more than two years ago. If you cannot afford a test, Berea cannot give you a Duolingo coupon, but it suggests asking your school to request one for you through the Duolingo website.

**If you want to study nursing:** the nursing major needs a higher score (TOEFL iBT 83, with 26 in speaking). You can be admitted with a lower score, but you cannot start the major until you reach it.

## When to apply

The deadline is **30 November**. All parts of your application must be in by then. Berea cannot extend the deadline. If you miss it, you can apply for the next academic year. You can only submit one application per year, and many students apply again after improving their English score.

Berea says it will confirm by email once your application is complete, and it will release final decisions **no later than 31 March**.

## How to apply

Everything is submitted through Berea's online application portal. Applying is free. Start from the official page: <https://www.berea.edu/admissions/admission-information/apply/checklist-items/international-faqs>

The checklist for international students is:

1. Online application
2. Copy of official secondary school records
3. Personal essay
4. One teacher recommendation
5. Financial resource recommendation
6. Official test scores
7. International Financial Questionnaire (Berea says you do not need to send bank statements)

The checklist page also lists an optional "personal submission". Berea says to give your **personal essay** very careful attention, because it helps the committee understand who you are, why you are applying and your career plans.

**If you are accepted:** Berea issues the I-20 (the form you need for your US student visa) only after you pay the deposit and send the required forms, such as your passport photo page and a medical history form. See our [student visa basics](/guides/student-visa-basics/) guide.

## Things to know

- Berea offers no English-language (ESL) classes. After you arrive, you take a placement exam to see whether you need one ESL course.
- Berea does not offer family housing or support. Your campus earnings cannot support family at home.
- Berea only gives a bachelor's degree. If you plan a professional programme afterwards (for example medicine), you must apply separately to that programme after Berea.

## What it is

The Chinese Government Scholarship is managed by the China Scholarship Council (CSC). Many countries first choose their nominees through a ministry or the Chinese embassy, and then those nominees apply on the CSC online portal.

## What we know, and what we do not know

We read Kenya's Ministry of Education notice for the 2026/27 intake as an example of how the process works. **Tanzania's process and dates may be different.** Please treat the Kenyan details below as an example, not as Tanzania's rules.

From the Kenyan notice:

- Bachelor's applicants should be **25 or under**, with strong grades in the subjects they want to study.
- For **Chinese-taught** programmes you need **HSK Level 3**. Programmes taught in English may ask for IELTS or TOEFL.
- **Bachelor's applicants must take the CSCA test** and submit the score report.
- Apply on the CSC portal (campuschina.org), choose the programme type shown in the notice, and also submit documents to the national ministry.
- Nominated candidates then face a written exam and an interview organised by the Chinese Embassy.

## What to do for Tanzania

1. Ask the **Ministry of Education, Science and Technology** and the **Chinese Embassy in Dar es Salaam** how Tanzanian students apply and when.
2. Look at the official CSC site for programme lists: <https://www.campuschina.org/>
3. Find out now whether you need HSK or the CSCA test, because tests have fixed dates and registration deadlines.

> **Careful:** the scholarship application does not need an agent. Some "agents" charge heavy fees for work you can do yourself. See [how to spot scams](/guides/how-scholarships-work-and-how-to-spot-scams/).

## What it is

The Global Korea Scholarship for Undergraduates (GKS-U) is run by Korea's National Institute for International Education (NIIED). It pays for a bachelor's degree in Korea, starting with a year of Korean language study.

## Two ways to apply

According to a September 2026 report in the Korea JoongAng Daily:

- **Embassy track.** You apply through the Korean embassy in your country, for bachelor's programmes. You choose up to three universities, and at least one must be a regional ("Type B") university.
- **University track.** You apply directly to one university and one department. The programmes are for science and engineering (the University Industry Cooperation programme, open to applicants from all countries) and a small associate-degree programme.

## Who can apply

- Under 25, meaning born after 1 March 2002.
- Graduated or expected to graduate from high school.
- Your parents must not hold Korean citizenship, and you must not hold it either.
- Graduates of Korean high schools, past GKS scholars and past recipients of a Korean government degree scholarship cannot apply.
- Grades must be given on an accepted scale, or you must provide an official explanation and a converted grade.

## What it covers

Tuition, the Korean language programme fee, return airfare and a monthly allowance. The report quotes about 14.4 million won a year during the degree. This allowance covers living costs, housing, health insurance and a settlement allowance.

## We share information, nothing more

Globe Stacks gives general information to help students learn about scholarships and applications. **We are not a scholarship provider, a university, an embassy or an agency.** We cannot apply on your behalf, and we cannot guarantee that you will win a scholarship or get a visa.

## Always confirm on the official page

Scholarship dates, rules and fees change often. We read official sources and show the date we last checked, but **you must confirm every detail on the official website before you apply or pay anything.**

## Links to other websites

We link to official websites. We do not control them and are not responsible for their content.

## Never pay for a promise

We never ask for money. If someone asks you to pay to "guarantee" a scholarship or visa, it is likely a scam. Read our [guide on scams](/guides/how-scholarships-work-and-how-to-spot-scams/).

## Corrections

If you find an error, please [contact us](/contact/) and we will correct it.

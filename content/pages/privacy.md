**Short version:** Globe Stacks does not ask you to create an account, and it does not collect your name, phone number or email through the website.

## What we store

- **On your device only:** the site may save your light or dark mode choice, and pages for offline reading. This stays on your phone or computer.

## What we do not do

- We do not run advertising on the site at this time.
- We do not sell personal information.

## If you contact us

If you message us on WhatsApp or email, we see what you send us. We use it only to reply to you or to improve the site.

## Changes

If we add advertising, analytics or sign-ups in the future, we will update this page first.

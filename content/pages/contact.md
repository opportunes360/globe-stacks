Use WhatsApp for quick questions, and email if you want to send documents or longer messages.

## What to write to us about

- A question about one of our guides.
- A mistake or an out-of-date date.
- A scholarship you would like us to add. Send the official link.

## Please remember

We cannot apply for you, and we never ask for money. If someone uses our name to ask for payment, please tell us.

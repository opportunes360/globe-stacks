Across Africa, many students get their academic dreams cut short. It is not because they lack ability. It is because of money, and because nobody explained how scholarships really work.

Globe Stacks started to change that. It was begun by a Form Six graduate who decided to learn how scholarships work and then share it in plain language, for free.

## What we do

- **Guides:** step-by-step help with personal statements, recommendation letters, passports and visas.
- **Scholarships:** undergraduate opportunities, with dates, rules and official links in one place.
- **Offline reading:** the site installs like an app and saves pages, so you can read with weak internet.

## How we work

- We read the **official pages** ourselves. Each page shows the date it was last checked.
- We say clearly when something is **not confirmed**, and we tell you who to ask.
- We list **fully funded and partly funded** options, plus loans, because one door closing should not end your plan.
- We are **not an agency**. We do not take fees, and we do not take part in applications for you.

## Found a mistake?

Details change quickly. If you see something out of date or wrong, please tell us on [WhatsApp](/contact/) or by email, and we will fix it.

Scholarship deadlines are spread across the whole year, and they are easy to miss. This guide puts them in one calendar so you can see what to do **this month**, what to do **next month**, and what you can no longer do.

Every date below comes from our scholarship pages, which we checked against the official websites on **18 November 2026**. Dates change, so always confirm on the official page before you act.

> **Read this first.** Many universities print only the day and month, not the year. We say so each time. When we write "not confirmed", it means the official page did not tell us.

## How to use this calendar

Find today's month below and do its tasks. If a date has passed, skip it. This calendar is for a student finishing Form Six who wants to start university in **2027**. If you are still in Form Five, jump to "If you finish Form Six in 2027" near the end.

**Not confirmed:** when your own ACSEE results are ready. Ask your school, because many applications need your results or a school report.

## November 2026: start now

- **Pick your targets.** Choose the scholarships you can still reach. Be honest about time.
- **Check the deadline today.** Berea (30 November) and UBC (2 December) are very close. Only start them if you can finish every part. Berea says it cannot extend its deadline.
- **Ask for letters.** Teachers need time. Read our guide on [recommendation letters and your CV](/guides/recommendation-letters-and-your-cv/).
- **Book your tests.** Yale requires SAT or ACT. Dartmouth does not say whether ACSEE counts (not confirmed), so the SAT or ACT is the safest route. Amherst says SAT or ACT is optional. NYU Abu Dhabi says it is test-optional through the 2027-2028 application cycle. Most places also ask for an English test unless you qualify for a waiver. We have not confirmed test dates or fees here.
- **Start your essay.** Use our guide on [how to write a personal statement](/guides/how-to-write-a-personal-statement/).
- **Check your passport.** If you do not have one, start now with [getting your passport in Tanzania](/guides/getting-your-passport-in-tanzania/).

## December 2026: finish the first round

- **1 December:** Yale aid documents, only if you applied through Early Action.
- **2 December:** UBC International Scholars. UBC says to submit well before the deadline in case of technical problems.
- **Chinese Government Scholarship:** bachelor's applicants must take the CSCA test before the application deadline. One government's list showed test dates of **19 December 2026** and **23 January 2027**. Check <https://www.csca.cn/> for dates and test centres you can reach from Tanzania.
- **Collect family papers.** For need-based aid at Yale, Dartmouth, Amherst and NYU Abu Dhabi you will need your family's income information. Each page tells you what to send.

## January 2027: the busiest month

This is the month when most US applications are due.

- **1 January:** Dartmouth. The page says 11:59 p.m. in your local time. One official page gives 2 January for a special group of applicants, so aim to finish well before 1 January.
- **2 January:** Yale.
- **5 January:** Amherst.
- **10 January:** NYU Abu Dhabi Early Decision II aid form, if you chose that round. Early Decision is **binding**: if you are admitted, you must go there. Think carefully.
- **10 January:** Türkiye Burslari is expected to open, but the 2027 dates are not confirmed. Watch the official site from late December. Last year's announcement came out on 29 December 2025.
- **12 January:** Oxford sends offers for 2027 entry.
- **15 January:** Amherst aid application.
- **26 January:** Reach Oxford. You can only apply if you have an Oxford offer. Oxford's own deadline for 2027 entry was 15 October 2026, so if you missed it, your next chance is 2028 entry.
- **31 January:** UBC documents are due.
- **CSC for China:** expect Tanzania's deadline in late January. This is **not confirmed**.

Read our [scam guide](/guides/how-scholarships-work-and-how-to-spot-scams/) before paying anyone. You do not need an agent to apply.

## February 2027: aid forms and waiting

- **1 February:** Dartmouth aid documents and the NYU Abu Dhabi Regular Decision aid form.
- **15 February:** Yale aid priority date. Yale says a late aid file will not reduce your offer, but finish early anyway.
- **20 February:** Türkiye Burslari is expected to close (not confirmed for 2027).
- **Keep your phone and email active.** UBC may invite some applicants to a video interview between January and March, and you only get one week to record your answers.
- **Check your spam folder** every few days. UBC says to mark its emails as safe.

## March 2027: results begin

- **Late March:** Yale decisions, and Amherst decisions. Dartmouth decisions come in late March or early April.
- **By 31 March:** Berea says it will release final decisions no later than 31 March.
- **MEXT Japan:** the last round opened in mid to late April, so start checking the Embassy of Japan in Tanzania's page in March. This is a pattern, not a date.

## April 2027: more results, and MEXT

- **1 April:** NYU Abu Dhabi Regular Decision results.
- **Early to mid April:** UBC says you will know by then.
- **Mid-April to May:** MEXT applications usually open. The 2027 round ran from 21 April to 21 May 2026, with the written exam on 3 June 2026 and the interview on 23 June 2026. Both exam dates were "subject to change". The next round is **not announced**.
- **Oxford selection** for the Reach scholarship takes place in April.
- **Egypt scholarships:** watch the Ministry of Education page from about April. The 2027/28 call is not confirmed.

## May 2027: decide and reply

- **1 May:** Yale and Dartmouth ask you to reply by this date. UBC's housing application is also due on 1 May (UBC asks you to wait for the award decision first).
- **By the end of May:** Oxford tells Reach applicants the result.
- **Morocco (AMCI):** Tanzania's list gives May to July as the usual application window. The 2024/25 call gave only a few weeks, so have your documents ready. Not confirmed for 2027/28.
- **If you have several offers,** compare what each one covers and what it does not, such as flights.

## June and July 2027: documents and visas

- **Win or not, keep going.** If you get an offer, you will need a [student visa](/guides/student-visa-basics/). Read it before you pay for anything.
- **Prepare your documents.** Yale says documents not in English need a certified translation, and it does not accept translations you make yourself.
- **Keep applying** to scholarships that are still open. The next rounds of Stipendium Hungaricum, CyprusAid and Mastercard partners may open at any time. None has an announced date.

## August and September 2027: get ready to travel

- **Türkiye Burslari** results are expected in early August, with initial procedures in August and travel in September (a pattern, not confirmed for 2027).
- **GKS Korea:** the 2027 round has closed. The next round has not been announced. The last one published its guidelines on 9 September 2026.
- **Do not book your own flight yet.** If an award includes travel support, as NYU Abu Dhabi's may, the university books it for you.

## If you finish Form Six in 2027

Then you are applying for **2028 entry**. No date for 2028 is announced yet, but this year's dates show the pattern. Use this as a plan, **not as confirmed dates**.

| This year | Expect something similar next year |
| --- | --- |
| Oxford application deadline 15 October | October 2027 |
| UBC deadline moved from 15 November to 2 December | Late 2027. UBC has not announced 2028 dates |
| Berea 30 November | Late November 2027 |
| Dartmouth, Yale, Amherst in the first week of January | Early January 2028 |
| Türkiye Burslari 10 January to 20 February | January to February 2028 |
| MEXT mid-April to May | April to May 2028 |

If you are in Form Five, start your personal statement early and gather your certificates. Preparing slowly is easier than rushing in December.

## Your checklist

- [ ] I know today's date and which deadlines have already passed.
- [ ] I picked 3 to 5 scholarships I can really finish.
- [ ] I wrote each deadline in my phone, with a reminder one week before.
- [ ] I asked two teachers and a counsellor for letters.
- [ ] I booked my SAT, ACT or English test if I need one.
- [ ] I wrote my personal statement and someone read it.
- [ ] I have a valid passport, or I have started the process.
- [ ] I collected my certificates, results and family income papers.
- [ ] I checked which scholarships need a CSS Profile or other aid form.
- [ ] I used only official web addresses and paid no agent.
- [ ] I checked my email and spam folder this week.
